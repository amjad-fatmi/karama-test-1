How You Can Help

Why Donate?

Your gift goes straight to books, groceries, training and the events that bring us together.

Where Your Gift Goes

  • English classes: textbooks, workbooks and classroom supplies
  • Food pantry: groceries, halal protein and baby supplies
  • Job training: certification fees, tools and coaching
  • Community events: food and supplies for heritage nights

Karama is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
